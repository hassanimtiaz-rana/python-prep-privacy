Python Prep Privacy Policy

Toolify Ai Studio · Effective 10 November 2026
Package: com.toolifyaistudio.pythonprep

Learning records

Python Prep stores lesson completion, bookmarks, review schedules, appearance preferences, and quiz history on your device. There is no account, advertising, developer analytics, purchase system, or hosted learning-data service in this release.

AI educational material

The AI modules are educational content. This release does not send prompts to an AI provider, run a live AI tutor, or execute your Python code.

External services

Opening a reference or support link launches an external service whose privacy practices apply. Standalone Android releases include Google Play in-app update support. Google Play and its update library process update-related app and device information, including information used for update availability, service operation, and diagnostics, under Google policies. Review Google Play in-app updates for more information. Expo Go development sessions also communicate with the development server and use Expo services; their behavior differs from a standalone release.

Retention and deletion

Learning records remain on this device until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Android backup is disabled in this configuration. There is no developer-side learning record to delete. Support emails may contain information you choose to share and are used to respond to support or privacy requests. Do not include passwords, tokens, or private code. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention required to handle the request or comply with applicable obligations.

Changes

This page will be updated if app data practices change, with a revised effective date. Review the policy associated with the version you use.
